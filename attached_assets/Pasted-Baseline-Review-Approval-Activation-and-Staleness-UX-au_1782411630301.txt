## Phase 1 — Read-only draft preview and comparison

Implement a governed, read-only preview for a draft weather-adjusted baseline.

Requirements:

* Draft preview must use the draft baseline’s exact stored snapshot and source basis.
* It must persist nothing.
* It must not change draft, approved, active, superseded, invalid, or baseline validation state.
* It must not write expected points, provenance, rollups, telemetry, project facts, weather mappings, or audit events.
* It must not appear in the normal O&M expected series as active operational truth.
* It must be visually labeled:
  “Draft preview — not approved or active.”
* It must preserve honest gaps, null expected values, missing inputs, invalid states, and period-effective boundaries.
* It must never fabricate a complete curve where inputs are unavailable.

The comparison must show:

* active baseline versus draft baseline;
* field-level diff;
* proposed curve overlay;
* explicit effective-date boundary;
* periods with unavailable/partial expected output;
* source-basis and provenance differences;
* reviewer-supplied constants distinctly from fact-backed values.
