Required policy refinement:

A weather declaration may clear a blocking weather dependency only when all of the following are true:

1. The declaration is physics-usable for the active weather-adjusted model.
2. The required metric semantics are complete:

   * irradiance basis is appropriate, such as POA where the model requires POA;
   * temperature type is appropriate, such as cell temperature where required;
   * sensor role and coverage are established.
3. The declaration basis is either:

   * `provider_confirmed`, or
   * `source_document`.
4. The declaration is active, current, and not stale/re-review-required.
5. Calibration/status meets the approved policy for that sensor type.

`reviewer_source_note` and `reviewer_assumption` must remain valid governed records, but they should not automatically clear a production-grade weather dependency or make a weather source physics-usable unless a later policy explicitly authorizes that outcome.
