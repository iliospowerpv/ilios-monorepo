Mercer County Italian American Festival Announces 2026 Entertainment Schedule

Three-day festival at Mercer County Park will feature Italian music, tribute acts, pop favorites, dancers, comedy, and family-friendly performances

MERCER COUNTY, NJ — The Mercer County Italian American Festival will return to Mercer County Park on Friday, September 18, Saturday, September 19, and Sunday, September 20, 2026, bringing a full weekend of Italian culture, food, family activities, and live entertainment.

This year’s entertainment lineup will feature a mix of Italian-American performers, vocalists, tribute acts, dancers, comedy, Motown, 1960s favorites, and pop music tributes. The festival stage will offer something for every generation, from classic Italian entertainment to popular tribute performances.

Friday, September 18, 2026

The festival opens Friday evening with a lineup of family-friendly entertainment and tribute performances:

5:15 p.m. — Bella Productions: Italian Skits
6:00 p.m. — Nigel Wells: Vocalist / Motown
6:30 p.m. — Flower Power: 1960s Review
7:30 p.m. to 9:00 p.m. — Lover: Taylor Swift Tribute to the Eras Tour

Saturday, September 19, 2026

Saturday evening will feature Italian music, pop vocals, a Jackie Wilson tribute, and a special national anthem performance:

6:00 p.m. to 7:00 p.m. — Tre Bella: Italian Trio
7:00 p.m. to 8:00 p.m. — The Chicletts: All-Girl Pop Trio
8:00 p.m. to 9:15 p.m. — Bobby Wilson & The Coda Band: Jackie Wilson Tribute
9:20 p.m. — Samantha Hutchins: National Anthem
9:30 p.m. — Fireworks Spectacular

Sunday, September 20, 2026

Sunday will continue the celebration with Italian dancers, vocal performances, Motown, Sinatra favorites, and Italian-American entertainment:

4:30 p.m. to 5:00 p.m.  — Italian Dancers
5:00 p.m. to 5:30 p.m. — Samantha Hutchins: Vocalist
5:30 p.m. to 6:00 p.m. — Nigel Wells: Motown Tribute
6:00 p.m.  to 7:00 p.m. — Sonny Averona: Sinatra Tribute
7:00 p.m.  to 8:00 p.m. — Joanna & Lewis: Italian-American Entertainment

Lewis Vanario, known for his role as Crazy Mario in A Bronx Tale and appearances in other films, is scheduled to appear as part of the Sunday entertainment lineup.

“The Mercer County Italian American Festival is proud to bring together music, food, culture, and family entertainment in one of the area’s most anticipated community celebrations,” said Al Hutchins. “This year’s entertainment schedule offers a great mix of Italian tradition, classic hits, tribute performances, and fun for all ages.”

Entertainment sponsorship opportunities are available for businesses, organizations, and individuals interested in supporting the festival and helping bring live entertainment to the community.

The Mercer County Italian American Festival will take place at Mercer County Park from September 18 through September 20, 2026.
