You are the Replit agent in the Ilios codebase. Implement a new “Sales” module to manage early-stage discovery and pipeline workflows, and connect it into the lifecycle so modules progressively activate from Sales → Due Diligence → Implementation/Asset Mgmt → Placed In Service → O&M.

CRITICAL LIMITATIONS
- “Project” remains the canonical physical asset record (formerly Site). No new entity for “Project.”
- Sales module will manage pipeline stages BEFORE formal due diligence. Keep it lightweight.

- Do not modify Operations telemetry systems; only link/activate based on state.

REQUIRED OUTCOME

DATA MODEL (MINIMAL)
Add the minimum fields to the canonical Project record (formerly Site) OR add a small related table if needed:
- salesStage (enum; include Discovery as earliest stage)
- source (optional: broker, inbound, developer, outreach, etc.)
- targetCloseDate (optional)
- probability (optional)
- notes (optional)
- assignedOwnerUserId (optional)
- pipelineValue (optional; ballpark)
- nextActionDate + nextActionNotes (optional)

Do NOT add complex contact management unless it already exists.

SALES STAGES (INITIAL)

- Discovery
- Qualified
- LOI / Term Sheet
- Under Contract
- Handoff to Due Diligence (this triggers or enables Diligence module focus)
